Time Series Reversal via End-of-Month Payment Cycle is a mean reversion strategy from Graziani, G. (2024). Universe: SPY, S&P 500 index or equivalent ETF; rebalances monthly. Entry: Buy S&P 500 at the start of the next month if the return of the last week of the current month is negative. Exit: Exit (sell) the S&P 500 position at the end of the holding month (i.e., hold for one full calendar month then close).
