Moving Average Leverage Timing Strategy is a trend following strategy from Gayed and Bilello, 2016. Universe: S&P 500 index or SPY, UPRO (3x leveraged S&P 500 ETF), SSO (2x leveraged S&P 500 ETF)...; rebalances daily. Entry: Buy leveraged equity exposure (e.g., 2x or 3x leveraged S&P 500 ETF or margin) when the broad U.S. equity market index (S&P 500) closes above its N-day simple moving average.
